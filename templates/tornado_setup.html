Set up the automation (iPhone)

  1. Open Shortcuts → the Automation tab → +.
  2. Choose App, pick TikTok, and check Is Opened.
  3. Select Run Immediately and turn Notify When Run off. Without this it just pings you instead of hijacking the screen.
  4. Tap Next → search for the Open URLs action and add it.
  5. Paste the URL above into it, then tap Done.
  6. Open TikTok. You should be thrown straight into the storm.

The counter runs on a rolling 90-minute window and lives in Safari's storage on your phone. Stay off TikTok for 90 minutes and you drop back to a breeze.

What this can and can't do

It can't draw on top of TikTok. iOS doesn't allow any app to paint over another one, so nothing short of a jailbreak puts a real tornado over the feed. What it does instead is get in front of you first — the automation fires the moment TikTok opens and Safari takes the screen.

You can escape it. Swipe back to TikTok and the storm is behind you. But the automation fires again the next time you open the app cold, and level 4 is a lot less fun than level 1. The friction is the point, not a lock.

Clearing Safari data resets the level. If you're doing that to dodge a 90-second wait, the tornado has already told you something useful.
